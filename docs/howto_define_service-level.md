## Service levels

For the language resources offered in the Language Bank of Finland one of three different levels of support can be defined:

    A: The resource is under active development. The Language Bank of Finland fixes any issues as soon as possible.
    B: The resource is developed only upon user request. The Language Bank of Finland aims to fix issues concerning the resource, but external contributions may be required.
    C: The resource is available ”as is”. The Language Bank of Finland does not fix nor develop the resource. External contributions are essential and welcome.
